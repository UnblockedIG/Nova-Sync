Nova-Sync is a free scrcpy web tool for your chromium browser

why would you want this?

well you can easily access your phone during school, work, or other place your phone isnt allowed just connect a high speed usb cable from each device.

how to setup

enable developer options on your phone usually by tapping the build number a certain amount of times

then scroll down or search for usb debugging turn it on

connect the cables and follow the instructions on the website. 

press allow on phone and then press mirror
